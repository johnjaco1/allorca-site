The problem

The buyers of private funds changed. Their diligence teams didn't.

Large endowments have analysts who read every page a manager sends. The family offices, wealth advisors and smaller institutions now buying private funds usually don't, and the history of each manager relationship lives in an inbox.

Manager memory

Drift is easy to miss one quarter at a time.

Each letter on its own looks reasonable. Allocra reads them against each other, and against what the manager promised in the DDQ and on your calls.

  • Compares every new document with the full history
  • Flags when a manager moves past what they told you
  • Keeps the record when an analyst leaves

Questions

Common questions

What documents can I upload?

Pitch decks, DDQs, quarterly letters, capital account statements and your own meeting notes, as PDFs.

Does Allocra make investment recommendations?

No. It reads, summarizes and flags issues with citations. Your team makes every investment decision.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Customer documents are never used to train models.

How is this different from a general AI chatbot?

Allocra keeps each manager's full history, compares new documents against it, writes in your template, and cites every claim.
