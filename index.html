The problem

The buyers of private funds changed. Their diligence teams didn't.

Large endowments have analysts who read every page a manager sends. The family offices, wealth advisors and smaller institutions now buying private funds usually don't. Each fund still sends hundreds of pages a year, and the history of each manager relationship lives in an inbox.

Questions

Common questions

What documents can I upload?

Pitch decks, DDQs, quarterly letters, capital account statements and your own meeting notes, as PDFs.

Does Allocra make investment recommendations?

No. It reads, summarizes and flags issues with citations. Your team makes every investment decision.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Customer documents are never used to train models.

How is this different from a general AI chatbot?

Allocra keeps each manager's full history, compares new documents against it, writes in your template, and cites every claim.
